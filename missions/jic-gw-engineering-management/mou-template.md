## 1. Purpose & Background (الغرض والخلفية)

**EN:**
This Memorandum of Understanding ("MOU") establishes a strategic partnership between Jeddah International College ("JIC") and [Employer Name] ("Employer") to facilitate the enrollment of Employer's engineering professionals in the George Washington University ("GW") Master of Science in Engineering Management program ("Program"), for which JIC holds exclusive Kingdom-wide onboarding rights.

**AR:**

**Background:**
- Vision 2030 and the National Industrial Strategy require Saudi engineering leadership
- The Program is 100% online, part-time, designed for working professionals
- JIC provides localized admissions support, Arabic-language advising, and cohort management
- Employer seeks to upskill engineering talent into management roles without losing them to competitors
