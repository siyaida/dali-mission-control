# 🎯 Paid-Ads Creative Brief — JIC × GW Engineering Management

**Platforms:** Google Ads + Meta (Facebook/Instagram) + Snapchat + TikTok  
**Regions:** Central (Riyadh) · Western (Jeddah) · Eastern (Dammam/Khobar) · Northwest (Tabuk) · South (Abha/Jazan)  
**Languages:** Arabic (primary) + English (secondary)  
**Goal:** Drive landing page visits → consultations → applications → enrollments

---

## Campaign Architecture

### Funnel Stages

| Stage | Goal | KPI | Budget Split |
|-------|------|-----|-------------|
| Awareness | Reach engineers, build brand | CPM, Reach, Video Views | 30% |

- Job Titles: Engineer, Senior Engineer, Project Manager, Engineering Manager
- Education: Bachelor's degree+

**Custom Audiences:**
- Website visitors (last 30/60/90 days)

**Lookalike Audiences:**
- 1% LAL of enrolled students

## A/B Test Plan

| Test | Variable | Variants |
|------|----------|----------|
| Hook | Opening frame | Problem-first vs. Aspiration-first |
| CTA | Button text | "Book Consultation" vs. "Get Started" vs. "Learn More" |
| Social Proof | Evidence type | GW ranking vs. Salary data vs. Alumni quote |
| Urgency | Scarcity signal | "Cohort 1" vs. "Limited seats" vs. "Application deadline" |
| Language | Primary language | AR-only vs. EN-only vs. Bilingual |
| Visual | Hero image | Engineer at desk vs. Giga-project site vs. GW campus |

**Duration:** 2 weeks per test  
**Minimum spend:** $500 per variant for statistical significance <!-- UNVERIFIED -->
