## What We Are Selling

## ICPs (Ideal Customer Profiles)

### B2C — Individual Engineers

#### 1. The Stuck Senior Engineer
- **Age:** 30–40
- **Pain:** 10+ years technical, plateaued, passed over for management roles
- **Aspiration:** Title bump, salary jump, leadership credibility
- **Location:** Riyadh, Jeddah, Dammam, Khobar
- **Trigger:** Passed up for promotion, new giga-project announced, peer got MBA

#### 2. The Giga-Project Climber
- **Age:** 28–38
- **Pain:** Technical enough to get hired, not credentialed enough to lead
- **Aspiration:** NEOM/Red Sea/Qiddiya leadership role
- **Location:** Anywhere mega-projects are hiring
- **Trigger:** Project announcement, recruiter call, LinkedIn post

#### 3. Military-to-Civilian Pivot
- **Age:** 35–50
- **Pain:** Military engineering background, not recognized in civilian HR systems
- **Aspiration:** Translate defense engineering into private sector leadership
- **Location:** Nationwide, especially Riyadh/Taif
- **Trigger:** Discharge planning, peer transition success story

#### 4. Family-Business Heir
- **Age:** 28–40
- **Pain:** Expected to take over family contracting/manufacturing firm, no formal management training
- **Aspiration:** Credibility + network to modernize the family business
- **Location:** Jeddah, Riyadh, regional cities
- **Trigger:** Father retiring, competitor moves ahead, Vision 2030 pressure

#### 5. Female Tech Leader
- **Age:** 25–40
- **Pain:** Technical ceiling harder for women in Saudi engineering — need credentials that transcend bias
- **Aspiration:** Break into leadership in a male-dominated field
- **Location:** Riyadh, Jeddah, Eastern Province
- **Trigger:** Saudiization mandates, quota openings, peer advancement

### B2B — Organizations

#### 1. HR/L&D at Top-50 Employers
- **Pain:** Engineering retention is terrible, leadership pipeline is empty
- **Aspiration:** Upskill senior engineers into managers without losing them to competitors
- **Trigger:** Turnover spike, new giga-project contract, Saudiization targets

#### 2. HRDF (Human Resources Development Fund)
- **Pain:** Fund training programs that actually place engineers in leadership
- **Aspiration:** Measurable ROI on training spend
- **Trigger:** Budget cycle, ministerial KPI review

#### 3. Tuwaiq Academy / NTDP / Misk
- **Pain:** Bootcamps create junior engineers, not leaders
- **Aspiration:** Layer a management credential on top of technical training
- **Trigger:** Program review, employer feedback

#### 4. MCIT / SDA / Saudi Council of Engineers
- **Pain:** Digital/tech/manufacturing strategy needs engineering managers, not just coders
- **Aspiration:** Workforce readiness for Vision 2030
- **Trigger:** Policy release, sector report

#### 5. Federation of Saudi Chambers / Regional Chambers
- **Pain:** SME members can't compete for giga-project contracts without credentialed management
- **Aspiration:** Chamber-sponsored upskilling programs
- **Trigger:** New project announcement, member survey

## الشرائح المستهدفة

### B2C — المهندسون الأفراد

1. **المهندس الكبير العالق** — 10+ سنوات تقنية، توقف، تم تجاوزه في أدوار الإدارة
2. **متسلق المشاريع الضخمة** — تقني بما يكفي للتوظيف، غير معتمد بما يكفي للقيادة

1. **الموارد البشرية / التعلم والتطوير في أفضل 50 شركة**
2. **صندوق تنمية الموارد البشرية (HRDF)**

4. **وزارة الاتصالات / هيئة البيانات / الهيئة السعودية للمهندسين**
