## Executive Summary

This document profiles the **Top 50 Anchor Employers** across Saudi Arabia’s five regions, identified as prime targets for GW Engineering Management graduate intake. These organizations are selected based on:

- Scale (employee count / growth trajectory)
- Strategic alignment with Vision 2030
- Giga-project involvement
- Engineering talent demand
- PIF backing or sovereign mandate
